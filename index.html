The Blocker — Support

The Blocker blocks porn, distracting sites, ads, trackers and malware in Safari on iPhone and iPad. Private, on-device, no account.

Blocking is not working?

  1. iOS 26: open Settings ▸ Apps ▸ Safari ▸ Extensions, tap The Blocker, then turn on Allow Extension.
  2. iOS 17–18: open Settings ▸ Apps ▸ Safari ▸ Extensions (iOS 17: Settings ▸ Safari ▸ Extensions) and, under Allow These Content Blockers, turn on The Blocker.
  3. Open The Blocker — the Home card should say "Safari blocking is on". Tap Test it, or open the blocking test in Safari.

Pro, purchases and refunds

Restore a purchase in The Blocker ▸ Settings ▸ Restore Purchases. Manage or cancel a subscription in Settings ▸ Apple Account ▸ Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
