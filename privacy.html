The Blocker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Your blocking happens on your device.

  • No accounts. You never sign up or log in.
  • No ads and no tracking. The app has no advertising SDKs, uses no advertising ID and shares nothing with other companies.
  • Anonymous usage data (version 2.8 and later). To see where setup gets stuck and which screens help, the app sends anonymous steps — for example "opened the paywall" or "turned on Safari blocking" — to our own server (apps.hossamhamouda.com), together with the app and iOS version, device type (iPhone or iPad), language and App Store country. Each install has a random ID; the copy on your phone is deleted with the app or when you turn this off. The steps never include the sites you block, links, anything you type, your name, email or IP address; they are not linked to you and not used for tracking. Only if you buy Pro while this is on, the purchase carries the same random ID at Apple, so Apple tells our server what happens to that purchase (plan, price, App Store country, and events like trial, renewal, cancellation or refund) — never your Apple Account or payment details. Data is deleted after 180 days. Turn it off anytime in Settings ▸ Share anonymous usage.
  • Your rules stay with you. Sites, categories, blocklists, schedules and the allowlist are stored in the app's private container on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync (Pro), they are copied to your own private iCloud Key-Value store and nowhere else.
  • Blocklist subscriptions are downloaded directly from the list URL you chose (for example StevenBlack/hosts on GitHub). That server sees a normal download request from your device; The Blocker sends it nothing else.
  • Safari content blocker. The extension gives Safari a rule list. Apple's design means the extension cannot see which pages you visit.
  • Activity stats. The optional Safari web extension ("The Blocker Stats") counts blocked navigations to show your Activity tab and, if you joined the 30-day challenge, shows a rescue page in place of a blocked site. Those counts and times stay on your device; anonymous usage data includes only a rough daily range (for example "1–5").
  • 30-day challenge. Your start day, your reason, the days you mark as a slip, your answers and the times of blocked attempts are stored only on your device. They power your progress, the rescue page and insights. Your reason and answers are never sent; anonymous usage data only notes that you joined, restarted, marked a slip or left, and on which challenge day.
  • Purchases. The Blocker Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. The app only receives a signed receipt saying whether Pro is active; it never sees your payment details. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends; manage them in Settings ▸ Apple Account ▸ Subscriptions.
  • Face ID / Touch ID is handled by iOS. The app only learns whether the unlock succeeded.
